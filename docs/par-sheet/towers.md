## The rule

8 rows; each row's traps are a shuffle of its tiles by three of the round's floats. After k safe rows the multiplier is 0.96 × (tiles / safe tiles)^k, floored to 0.0001×; the round cashes out by itself at the top or before a row would pass 5,000×. A player who cashes out after k rows has RTP = P(k safe rows) × multiplier.
