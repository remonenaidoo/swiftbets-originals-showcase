# Kimberley Hold: maths document

Kimberley Hold is a theme on the Highveld Hold engine: `OneShotGames.All` registers it as `new HoldSlot("kimberley-hold", "Kimberley Hold")`, the same class with the same strips, pays, feature and 5,000× cap. Only its id, its name and its page's art differ. Its RTP, hit rate and simulation are therefore Highveld Hold's, in [highveld-hold.md](highveld-hold.md).
