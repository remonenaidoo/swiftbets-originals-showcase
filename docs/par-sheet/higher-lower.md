## The rule

Cards from an endless deck: each is the round's float × 52, floored (rank = card mod 13 + 1, Ace low). A guess of higher-or-same on rank r wins with 14 − r ranks of 13; lower-or-same with r. After winning guesses with counts c₁ … c_k the multiplier is 0.96 × 13^k ÷ (c₁ × … × c_k), floored to 0.0001×, which is 0.96 ÷ P(all k win). Skips change nothing. A guess that cannot lose, or whose win would pass 5,000×, is refused.

A player who cashes out after any sequence of winning guesses gets RTP = P × floor(0.96 ÷ P × 10,000) ÷ 10,000, which is at most 96% and loses less than 0.0001 × P to the flooring. Over every sequence of one or two guesses the RTP is between 95.9929 % and 96.0000 %; longer sequences are the same bound.
