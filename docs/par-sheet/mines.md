## The rule

25 tiles; the player chooses m mines (1–24). After k safe picks the multiplier is 0.96 × C(25, k) / C(25 − m, k), floored to 0.0001×. The chance of k safe picks is C(25 − m, k) / C(25, k), so a player who cashes out after k picks has RTP = chance × multiplier: 96% before flooring and the cap. The round cashes out automatically when every safe tile is revealed or when the next pick would pass the 5,000× cap, so no reachable multiplier is ever cut by the cap.
